Fields tab: row menus (read from the live ERP)

FieldTypeMenu

UPC, MPN, EAN and ISBN are the four built-in product identifier fields (barcode and part-number types). They are customizable fields (Edit and Configure Access are available) and appear in New Item under Add Identifier. Alias Name, MRP and Minimum Profit Percentage are built-in fields whose only actions are the ones listed. In the live account all three read Active when checked on 19 Nov 2026, while the first capture read them Inactive, so treat their state as unstable.
