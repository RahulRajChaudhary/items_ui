ERP Items: settings, modules, and what changes where

What this guide covers. Part 1 lists every setting under Settings > Items in the ERP. Part 2 shows what is inside each Items and Sales screen (tables, forms, and what an opened record shows). Part 3 shows what changes in the Items or Sales modules when a setting is changed, and the screen path where you see it. Checked on a trial account on 18 Nov 2026 as the Admin user. Test records created during checking were deleted afterwards.

PART 1: Items settings (Settings > Module Settings > Items)

Settings: every Items option, as it appears on screen

This mirrors Settings > Items in the ERP. Use the tab strip to move between its six tabs. Click any switch or value (blue outline) and the guide jumps to Part 3 and shows exactly what that change does in the Items or Sales module. Clicking here does not change your ERP. A green ON or grey OFF is the current state on this account.

Buttons: the blue button opens the settings page where you change it; the outlined buttons open the ERP screen where you see the effect. They work when you are signed in to the ERP in the same browser.
